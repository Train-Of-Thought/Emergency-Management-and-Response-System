ABOUT THE SYSTEM

A more organized approach to emergency management.

The Emergency Management and Response System is a proposed digital platform for Minglanilla, Cebu. It aims to provide an organized process for reporting, recording, monitoring, and coordinating emergency incidents.

HOW IT WORKS

A structured reporting process

01

04
